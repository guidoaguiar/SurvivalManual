**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Survival Manual app (the "Application") for mobile devices and the web, maintained by Guido Aguiar (the "Maintainer") as an open source project. The Application is provided "AS IS".

**No data collection**

The Application works entirely offline. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it does not require an account.

**Data stored on your device**

The Application stores the following only on your own device, to make it work:

*   Your settings (theme, text size, language, reading speed)
*   Your reading position, bookmarks and recently read chapters
*   Checklists you create
*   The emergency card, if you fill it in (for example name, blood type, allergies, medications and emergency contacts)

This data never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the Application's data or uninstalling it.

**Device permissions**

*   **Camera**: used only to switch the flashlight on and off for SOS signals. No photos or video are taken.
*   **Location**: used only to show true north on the compass. Your location is not stored or sent anywhere.

Both permissions are optional. The rest of the Application works without them.

**External links**

Some chapters contain links to external websites. If you open one, that website's own privacy policy applies.

**Children**

The Application does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

**Changes**

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published in this file in the project's repository.

**Contact**

For questions about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/guidoaguiar/SurvivalManual/issues.

This policy is effective as of 2026-10-16.
